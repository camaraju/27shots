# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated October 17, 2026**

This Privacy Notice for 27shots ("we," "us," or "our") describes how we handle information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

- Download and use our mobile application (27shots), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
- Use 27shots, a photo app that gives your photos the look of a disposable film camera. You can upload up to 10 photos from your photo library and develop them with a film look (FLASH or DAYLIGHT), or shoot a "roll" of up to 27 photos with your phone's camera that are developed and saved automatically to that roll.
- Contact us by email

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

## SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

**What personal information do we collect?** None through the app. 27shots does not require an account, and we do not collect, store, or have access to your photos or any other personal information from the app. If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message.

**Where are my photos?** Everything happens on your device. The film looks are applied on your phone, and your photos and rolls are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers or to anyone else.

**Do we process any sensitive personal information?** No.

**Do we collect any information from third parties?** No.

**Do we use analytics, advertising, or tracking?** No. 27shots contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking tools.

**Do we share personal information?** No. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.

**What are your rights?** Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. Because we do not collect personal information through the app, most of these rights will only apply to emails you send us. Learn more about your privacy rights below.

**How do you exercise your rights?** By emailing us at juliecamara@gmail.com.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

*In Short: We do not collect personal information through the app.*

**No account or registration.** 27shots does not ask you to create an account or log in. We do not ask for your name, email address, password, phone number, or any other personal details in the app.

**Your photos and rolls.** Photos you develop or take with 27shots, and the rolls you create (including the roll names you choose), are processed and stored only on your device. We do not receive, collect, or have access to them.

**No automatic collection.** The app does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage data, log data, diagnostic data, or crash reports, and it does not send any information to us or to third parties.

**Location.** 27shots does not request access to your location and does not collect location data.

**Information you send us by email.** If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you.

## 2. HOW DOES THE APP USE YOUR DEVICE'S CAMERA AND PHOTO LIBRARY?

*In Short: The app asks for permission to use your camera and photo library only so it can work. Everything stays on your device.*

With your permission, 27shots uses the following functions of your device:

- **Camera** — to take photos when you shoot a roll.
- **Photo library (read access)** — to let you choose up to 10 photos to develop with a film look.
- **Photo library (save access)** — to save the developed photos you choose to your device's photo library.

These permissions are used only on your device to provide the features above. You can allow or refuse each permission, and you can change your choice at any time in your device's Settings. If you refuse a permission, the related feature (for example, shooting a roll or uploading photos) will not be available, but the rest of the app will continue to work.

## 3. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

*In Short: Your photos are processed only on your device. The only personal information we may process is what you send us by email.*

- **Applying film looks.** When you develop a photo or shoot a roll, the film look is applied by the app on your device. No photo is sent to us or to any server.
- **Responding to you.** If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to your question or request.

## 4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

*In Short: We do not share your personal information.*

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties. The only exception is if we are required to do so by law.

**Sharing you choose to do.** If you choose to share a photo from your photo library (for example, by message or on social media), that sharing happens outside 27shots and is governed by the privacy policies of the service you use.

## 5. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

*In Short: No.*

27shots does not use cookies, web beacons, pixels, advertising identifiers, or any other tracking technologies. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

*In Short: Your photos and rolls stay on your device until you delete them. We keep emails only as long as needed to reply.*

- **Photos and rolls in the app** are stored on your device until you delete a roll or delete the 27shots app. Deleting the app removes all rolls stored inside it. Photos you have already saved to your device's photo library are not affected and remain there until you delete them yourself.
- **Emails you send us** are kept only for as long as needed to respond to you and handle your request, unless a longer period is required by law.

## 7. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

*In Short: Your photos never leave your device, which keeps them under your control.*

Because 27shots processes and stores your photos only on your device, your photos are protected by your device's own security features, such as your passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. We recommend keeping your device's software up to date and protected with a passcode. We cannot recover photos or rolls that are deleted from the app or lost if your device is lost, damaged, or reset.

## 8. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

*In Short: No. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.*

27shots can be used by people of all ages. The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If a child contacts us by email, we will use the information only to respond and will then delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, please contact us at juliecamara@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 9. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

*In Short: You are in full control of your photos, and you can ask us about any email you have sent us.*

**Your photos and rolls.** You can delete any roll at any time from within the app, and you can delete all app data by deleting the 27shots app from your device.

**Device permissions.** You can withdraw your permission for the app to use your camera or photo library at any time in your device's Settings.

**Information you sent us.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent us by email. To make a request, contact us at juliecamara@gmail.com.

## 10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Some web browsers and mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. Because 27shots does not track you, there is no tracking to turn off.

## 11. DO OTHER REGIONS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?

*In Short: You may have additional rights based on the country you reside in.*

### Australia and New Zealand

We handle personal information in accordance with the obligations and conditions set by Australia's Privacy Act 1988 and New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 (Privacy Act).

This Privacy Notice explains what personal information we collect, from which sources, for which purposes, and who receives it, as required by both Privacy Acts. As described above, the app does not collect personal information, and the only personal information we may receive is information you choose to send us by email.

At any time, you have the right to request access to or correction of any personal information we hold about you. You can make such a request by contacting us using the details in the section "HOW CAN YOU CONTACT US ABOUT THIS NOTICE?" below.

If you believe we are unlawfully processing your personal information, you have the right to submit a complaint about a breach of the Australian Privacy Principles to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and a breach of New Zealand's Privacy Principles to the Office of the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner.
